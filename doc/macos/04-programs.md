youtube music doesn't have a native app but can create one with Safari

1. Open Safari and go to YouTube Music.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Click File in the top menu bar of your screen.
4. Select Add to Dock.
5. Name the app (e.g., "YouTube Music") and click Add.
6. The app icon will now appear in your Dock and Launchpad for quick access.
